# ⭐ Star Catcher

A fast arcade game where you catch falling stars, avoid dangerous red stars, collect power-ups, and beat your high score.

[![Click to Play](https://img.shields.io/badge/CLICK%20ME%20TO%20PLAY-itch.io-orange?style=for-the-badge&logo=itchdotio)](https://ambariz.itch.io/the-star-catcher)

**LINK :** https://ambariz.itch.io/the-star-catcher

---

## Gameplay (See the Screenshots below!)

- Catch falling stars to increase your score
- Avoid red stars that reduce your lives
- Collect comet power-ups to activate **Big Hands Mode**
- Collect shield bubbles for protection against one hit
- Progress through increasing difficulty levels
- Try to beat your personal best score

---

## Controls

- **A / D** or **← / →** → Move Left & Right
- **SPACE** → Start Game
- **R** → Restart After Game Over

---

## Features

- Smooth HTML5 Canvas gameplay
- Progressive difficulty system
- High score saving using Local Storage
- Shield power-up system
- Big Hands power-up
- Level progression
- Sound effects and level-up audio
- Animated space background
- Game Over and Restart system
